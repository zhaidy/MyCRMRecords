# Request forms: the approved layout

The Request forms list now looks like the approved design, as the Costing sheets: the search, the type as a switch (All,
Delivery, Collection), Status and Sales rep are glass capsules, Created shows the creation dates, and each request form
is one line with its status and Pending print tags and its delivery or collection dates; click anywhere on the line to
open it.

A request form shows its type and status beside its number, its key details under the title, then the tabs Items and
Delivery (or Collection items) and More details, with History (as a timeline) and Attachments beside them. The print
button says Print delivery form or Print collection form.

On a phone, Orders switches between Costing sheets and Request forms at the top; each request form is a card, and on
its page each part is a card that opens and closes.
