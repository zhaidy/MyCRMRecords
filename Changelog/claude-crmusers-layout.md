# CRM users page: the approved layout

The CRM users page now looks like the approved design. The People and Teams tabs, the search, a new Role filter and
the Show everyone switch are glass capsules in one bar under the top bar. Each person is one line with their role,
teams, salesman code, territory code and number of territories, 50 to a page; Edit (or Add for someone who is not a CRM
user), or a click on the line, opens the person in a panel at the side, with the teams to pick as tiles, the details
in a grouped list, their territories and Remove CRM user and territories at the bottom.

Teams are one line each with an Active switch, the number of people (which opens who is in the team), People and Edit.

On a phone the people and teams are cards, People and Teams fill the width, the role is picked from a list that opens
from the bottom, and a person opens on the whole screen.
