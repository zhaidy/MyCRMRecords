# Dormant opportunities

A new page, **Dormant**, helps salespeople and managers follow up old prospects that might still be an opportunity. It lists, for your territories (a manager: the team's, with a choice of salesperson and territory), the prospects that were lost or have been left open with no activity for 90 days, and the companies visited beyond a first call that never got a prospect. Each row has a High, Medium or Low priority from the furthest the company got, says why it is listed, and opens to show the activity history.

For each one you record your decision: Followed up, No opportunity (with a reason) or Snooze until a day. The follow-up itself is still done in D365, so each row has a Copy name button. Download Excel (all) downloads everything in your scope, whatever the filters, with both lists, the full activity history and the decisions.

An administrator has to run the data migration "Dormant opportunities: decisions" on the Data migrations tab once.
