# Phone: page titles no longer hidden behind the page's buttons

On a phone, the title that shows at the top of the screen once you scroll (a company's name, Companies…) was covered by
the page's buttons at the top right, such as print and edit. The title now stops before those buttons, cut short with …
when it is long.

On Companies, **New company** is now the round + beside the bottom bar, as on Territories, Activities and Projects, so
it no longer takes room at the top of the screen.
