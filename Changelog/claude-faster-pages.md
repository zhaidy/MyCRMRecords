# Projects open in a moment; a Speed tab for administrators

- The Projects list no longer reads every company of the projects it shows: some of MyCRM's projects hold tens of
  thousands of companies. It counts them and names the first in one step ("31 BAR & KITCHEN +50,002 more").
- A project's page lists the first 300 companies added when it has more, and says how many it has; a member's temporary
  territory shows its first companies and how many more.
- Settings has a **Speed** tab: how long each kind of request took since the website started, the slowest first, against
  the aim of 0.3 seconds.
- Browsers keep MyCRMRecords' styles, scripts, icons and fonts for a week (a new version is still picked up at once).
