# The company page as designed

A company's Overview now shows the Invoice / SOA emails Advance uses for each contract (read only), the salesperson with their manager and the caretakers from open projects, the three latest activities, and under More company details the machines the company sold as a dealer. The tiles count machines, projects (with how many are open), activities (with how many are upcoming) and files.

Addresses are filtered with All, Billing, Delivery or Storage, each row with its type, Default, and edit and delete buttons. Contacts open with the arrow to show all their details; on a phone each contact has a call and a mail button. Related companies are grouped as Parent, Siblings and Subsidiaries with their territory and an add button each, and the Territory Movement History shows the previous and current sales rep and manager, who changed it and when.

The Files tab groups the files by their number, with Download all for a group; the Costing sheets and Request forms tabs say how many there are and open the full list of the company's. The printed summary lists the manager and caretakers too.
