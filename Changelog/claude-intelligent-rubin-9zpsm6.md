# Sign in as… from your menu, for Sales Admins too

Sign in as… has moved from the Companies and Costing sheets pages to your own menu: press your initials at the top right (on a phone, More) on any page. Sales Admins can now use it as well as administrators, to see MyCRMRecords as a salesperson sees it; they cannot sign in as an administrator. As before, everything done while signed in as someone is recorded as done by you on their behalf.
