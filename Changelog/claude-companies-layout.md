# Companies page: the new layout, Mine and No salesperson

The Companies page now looks like the approved design. The filters (search, Customer type, Group name, Postal code) are
glass capsules above the list, with Reset beside them. Each company is one line with its BRN, postal code, salesperson,
territory, customer type and creator; click the name or anywhere on the row to open it (the Open button is gone). The
line under the title gives the number of companies.

If you see every company, you can show All, Mine (your and your team's territories) or No salesperson (companies in no
permanent territory). Sales Admins and MD can tick companies with no salesperson and put them in a territory from there.

On a phone the companies are cards, the All / Mine / No salesperson switch fits the screen, and the other filters open
in a sheet from the round filter button beside the search.
