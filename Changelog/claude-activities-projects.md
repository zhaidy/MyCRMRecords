# Activities and Projects

MyCRM's sales activities and projects are now in MyCRMRecords, saved in MyCRM as before.

The new Activities page (in the menu after Companies and Projects; on a phone under More) lists your and your team's activities by day or as a month's calendar, with a search, Upcoming/Today/Past/All, the activity type and the owner, and downloads them to Excel. New activity opens MyCRM's form: company, action, remarks, dates and times, owner, completed, and the activity types, participants, project, attendees and related prospects. Only an activity's owner can change or delete it.

The new Projects page lists the projects you or your team are in; Managers, Admins, Sales Admins and MD create one in two steps. A project's page has its companies, members (with the temporary access each one gets), linked activities and history; the owner or a Sales Admin edits, closes or deletes it, and the owner adds and removes members. A member who does not own a company of the project gets a temporary territory with it while the project is open; it is taken away when they leave, the project closes or is deleted, or its end date passes (the scheduled task Projects that have ended, off until an administrator turns it on).

A company's page has three new tabs, Activities, Prospects (view only, with Open in MyCRM) and Projects, and a New activity button.
