# Old machines open, and the Company summary lists machines

A machine whose contract ended long ago, and which is no longer in Advance's list of machines in the field, now opens
from its company's Machines tab: its page shows its last contract, customer and history instead of "no machine". The
Machines page finds such a machine when you type its whole serial number.

The Machines tab's number (and the Machines tile on the Overview) now counts all four sections, expired machines and
machines without contract included.

The Company summary (Download on the Companies page) now also has each company's machines: four counts on the first
sheet, and a Machines sheet with a row for each machine, as the company's Machines tab shows them.
