# Machines: Advance's machines in MyCRMRecords

A new **Machines** page (in the menu after Files, and in the phone's bottom bar between Files and Orders) lists
Advance's machines of the companies you may open: search by serial number, model, company or BRN, and choose a
coverage (Contract, Warranty, T&M, Expired, No contract) and a finance type (Internal lease, External lease, Outright,
Dealer). Each row says how you see it: your territory, a project, or temporary access. The roles that open every
company see every machine once they type 3 characters.

A machine's page shows its contract, warranty, finance type, contact and location, the copies billed per month (6 or
12 months), its service history with meter readings, its toner orders and meter readings, and for a machine on time and
materials its last contract's rates.

A company's page has a new **Machines** tab, as MyCRM's: the machines whose contract or warranty is still running, or
all of them.

Sales Admins have a **Machine access** page listing MyMachines' temporary access. Giving and removing it works once an
administrator ticks "Let MyCRMRecords change MyMachines' temporary access" (Settings, MyCRM tab); a removed access is
kept in MyMachines, marked, never deleted.
