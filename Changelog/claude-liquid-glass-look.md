# A new look, and MyCRMRecords on your phone

Every page has a new, lighter look: the menu floats at the top in a see-through bar, the pages' buttons, tabs, search
and filters float above the lists, and the lists and forms sit on plain white cards (black in dark mode). Text uses
your device's own font.

On a phone the menu is now a bar at the bottom of the screen with Companies, Orders and More; More opens the rest of
the menu from the bottom.

MyCRMRecords can be installed as an app. On a computer, Install app in the top bar shows a QR code to open
MyCRMRecords on your phone and install it there; on a phone the same button installs it on the home screen. See
"Using MyCRMRecords on a phone" in the user guide.
