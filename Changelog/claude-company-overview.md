# A company's new Overview and summary print

The company page's Overview is rebuilt: the company's name large with one line saying its territory, salesperson, BRN
and customer code, and Print summary and Edit company together at the top right. The Overview shows the company
details, a new chart of the copies billed per month on all the company's machines over the last 12 months (black and
colour, from Advance), tiles counting its machines, files, contacts and costing sheets that open their tabs, the
salesperson, the sales notes (No of Employees and Sales Remarks), the addresses and more company details.

Print summary prints the company on one A4 page (or saves it as PDF): its details, salesperson, addresses, primary
contacts and the machines whose contract or warranty is running.

On a phone the company page shows its details and a list of sections, More details first.
