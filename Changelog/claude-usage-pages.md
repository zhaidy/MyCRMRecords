# A company's usage and history, and its machines by coverage

A company has two new pages, read only from Advance. Usage & history (from the Overview's Copies per month and the Machines tab) shows the black and white or colour copies billed on all the company's machines per month over 6 to 96 months or months you choose, each beside the same month a year before, as a chart and a table, and the copies of each year with how many machines were billed. Export to Excel downloads it as a workbook.

Grouped by coverage (from the Machines tab) lists the company's machines in groups: on contract (by contract), warranty, time and material (with Rates for the last contract), contract expired and no contract. Each group folds, and Collapse all folds them all.
