# Prospects page

A new **Prospects** page (in the menu next to Activities, and under Sales in More on a phone) lists your prospects in
MyCRM, or your team's, newest first. Search by company or remarks, and filter by Open, Closed, Cancelled or All, by
stage, by type, by owner and by estimated order date. Click a prospect to open its company on the Prospects tab. The
page is view only: prospects are still created and changed in MyCRM.
