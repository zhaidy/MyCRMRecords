# Companies, territories and CRM users move from MyCRM

MyCRMRecords now does MyCRM's Users, Territory and Company work. Companies: search the companies you may open, open a company to see its details, addresses, contacts with their phones and emails, parent and subsidiary companies and its territory movements; Sales Admins add and change companies and group names (a BRN another company has is refused, unless the company is a home user). Territories: Sales Admins (and MD) create named territories with a number, give each a salesperson and put companies in them, one by one, from a pasted list or from Excel; every change of salesperson is recorded. CRM users: Sales Admins set each person's MyCRM role, teams and codes, and keep the teams.

MyCRM keeps working with the same data: MyCRM and MyCRMRecords are kept in step every 10 minutes once an administrator switches the sync on. Advance's customer numbers are read every night by a scheduled task, once switched on.
