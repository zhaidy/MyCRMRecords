# A company's page in tabs

A company's page now shows its parts in tabs under the company's name: Overview, Contacts, Costing sheets, Request
forms and Relationships & history, each with how many there are. Clicking a tab shows it at once, without leaving the
page, and coming back from a costing sheet or request form opens the same tab.

On a phone the tabs are a list under the company's details; each opens on the whole screen, with the company's name at
the top to go back.
