# Costing sheets: the approved layout

The Costing sheets list now looks like the approved design: the search and the Status, Sales rep and Specialist
filters are glass capsules (Specialist now holds Has specialist / No specialist and the specialists in one list), Dates
shows the date filters, and each costing sheet is one line with a status tag; click anywhere on the line to open it.
Download and Sign in as are together at the top.

A costing sheet shows its key details under the title, then the tabs Product, Meter charges, Billing, Variable costs &
trade-ins and More details, with History (as a timeline) and Attachments beside them. Layout (New CS or Old CS) sits
beside Print.

On a phone, Orders opens a list with Costing sheets / Request forms at the top, the status in a list that opens from
the bottom and the other filters behind the round button; each costing sheet is a card, and on its page each part is a
card that opens and closes.
