# Company summary and projects downloads

The Companies page has a Download button: an Excel workbook (the Company summary, as MyCRM's report of that name) of every company that matches the search, filters and All, Mine or No salesperson, not just the page shown, with each company's salesperson, team and manager, territory, postal code and billing address, phone, website, caretake remarks and the caretakers of its open projects.

The Projects page has a Download button too: every project that matches Open, Closed or All and the search, and a second sheet with a line for each of their companies showing its account owner, the project's caretakers and whether they have temporary access, to check caretake accounts (MyCRM's Projects and Caretake Account reports).

Both pages also have a Print button and print cleanly (or save as PDF): the title, a line with the filters and the list, without the menus and filters. Downloads are recorded in the audit log.
