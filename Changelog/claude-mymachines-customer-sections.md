# Machines tab shows exactly what MyMachines showed

A company's Machines tab now shows the company's machines exactly as MyMachines' customer page did, in its four
sections: Contract machines (each running contract with its machines, BW and COL meter groups and free copies),
Warranty machines (with the extended warranty), Contract expired machines and Machines without contract.

Contract expired machines now come from Advance's contracts, so machines whose contract ended years ago, and which are
no longer in Advance's list of machines in the field, show again (before, such a company showed "Advance has no
machines with this BRN").

An administrator can check the new Advance tables on the admin page: Settings, Connections tab, Test for
AdvanceConnector (their names are under "Names in those databases").
