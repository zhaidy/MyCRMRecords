# The pages look like the designs

On a computer the top bar is now separate floating pieces, with the page showing between them: the menu with the
MyCRMRecords name, then four round buttons on the right. The new magnifying glass opens the Companies page with the
cursor in its search box; next to it are the user guide (?), Install app and your initials. On a narrower screen the
buttons keep only their icons instead of crowding the menu.

Pages are a little wider, with bigger text in lists and forms and every page title the same size. Buttons inside cards,
lists and windows are plain: soft blue for adding and editing, red words for Remove, small icons without a frame. Only
what floats over the page (the top bar, page actions, filters, tabs, windows, messages) keeps the glass look. Lists
reach the edges of their cards, tags are soft colours, and the warning on a costing sheet or request form is a soft
amber note with a warning sign. Messages at the bottom show a check and an OK button. Windows open at the top of the
screen.

On a phone there is no bar at the top any more: the page starts with its title, the back button floats at the top left
and the page's buttons at the top right (as icons). The user guide, Install the app, your name and Sign out are on the
More screen. Windows slide up from the bottom as a sheet. Fixed: on a company the print and edit buttons covered the
company's name, and on Projects the search box was squeezed beside Open / Closed / All.
