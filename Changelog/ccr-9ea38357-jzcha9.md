# Clearer Companies and company pages

On the Companies list, "No salesperson" stays on one line instead of breaking out of its label, and its text is darker so it is easier to read.

On a company's page, the Machines, Projects, Activities and Files labels in the tiles are darker and easier to read. The Black and Colour key of Copies per month shows only when there is a chart, and "No address." is the same size as the other cards' messages. On a phone, each section in the Sections list has its own picture, matching the More menu, instead of its first letter (Machines and More details both showed "M").
