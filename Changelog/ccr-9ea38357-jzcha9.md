# Easier to read, and bigger text when you ask for it

Blue buttons (New company, New activity, Save…) are now solid blue, so their white text is easier to read. In the dark theme, the selected tab or option is a deeper blue, and blue text and links are brighter. The grey hint text in search and filter boxes ("Company name or BRN", "Any") is darker, and the team names on CRM users are easier to read.

If you set a larger text size in your browser, MyCRMRecords now follows it; before, the text stayed the same size. With large text on a narrower screen, MyCRMRecords switches to the phone layout instead of letting the menu run into the buttons beside it. On a phone, the labels in the bottom bar are a little bigger, and No salesperson fits in the All / Mine / No salesperson choice.

Pressing Tab to reach the company search now shows the same blue outline as the filters beside it.
