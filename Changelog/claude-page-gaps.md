# Last service on Machines, a territory filter on Files, a territory's History

The Machines page shows each machine's last service (the day its last service call was completed), Machine access sits in a glass capsule beside the title, and the finance type is a labelled capsule like the other filters. The list is in one card with its pager inside.

A machine's history tabs stay under the top bar as you scroll, and its Meter readings show the copies billed each month (with the invoices of the month) and the monthly average.

The Files page shows each file's territory (its company's) and has a Territory filter (your and your team's territories) beside Uploaded; ticked files show "N selected" with Clear, and Download as zip.

A territory has a third tab, History: the companies moved to or from its salesperson, newest first, with who moved them.

On the Activities page the search and filters stay under the top bar as you scroll. A project's Members tab calls the column Temporary territory, and a deleted project's page says so, with the way back to Projects.
