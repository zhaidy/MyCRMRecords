# Dialogs and the phone's More, back and + buttons as in the design

Every window that opens over a page (forms, questions, Install app, Sign in as someone else, a machine's last contract)
now has the same glass head: a round × at the left closes it, the title is in the middle and the button that does the
work is at the right (Save, Create territory, Save changes; red for Take away, Delete and Remove). There is no Cancel
and Save row at the bottom any more.

On a phone, More in the bottom bar opens a More screen: your name and role, Install the app, the other pages in groups
(Sales, Records, Machines, App, showing only the pages your role opens) and Sign out. A record's page has a round back
button at the left of the top bar, and the page's title shows in the bar once you scroll past it. On Territories,
Activities, Projects and a company's page, the create button is a round + beside the bottom bar.
