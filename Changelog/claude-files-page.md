# Files: MyFile's PDFs in MyCRMRecords

A new **Files** page (in the menu after Request forms, and in the phone's bottom bar between Companies and Orders)
shows the PDFs kept in MyFile, newest first: costing sheets, request forms, FSAs, leasing papers, DOs and others.
Search by file name, invoice, DO, contract or service number, company or BRN; choose a type and how recently they were
uploaded. On a computer the chosen file's PDF shows beside the list, with Open full size and Download; ticked files
download together as a zip. On a phone each file is a card that opens its PDF on the whole screen.

A company's page has a new **Files** tab with its newest files and a link to all of them.

Everyone sees the files of the companies they may open and the files of no company; the roles that open every company
see every file. The files are only shown: nothing is uploaded or changed here.
