# Contacts' phones and emails in the Company summary

The Company summary (the Companies page's **Download**) now lists each company's contacts, the primary contact first, with their phones and emails in the new columns **Contacts**, **Contact phones** and **Contact emails**. Before, the only phone in the file was the company's own phone, which most companies leave empty because MyCRM keeps the numbers and email addresses on the contacts. A company like that looked as though it had no phone or email at all.

The company's own number is still there, in the column now called **Company phone**.
