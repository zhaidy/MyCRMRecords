# Territories page: the approved layout

The Territories page now looks like the approved design. The list is one line per territory with its number,
salesperson, number of companies and when it was created and by whom; All, Vacant and Taken away sit beside the search
(Taken away replaces the Show territories taken away box), and a click anywhere on a line opens the territory. New
territory lets you search the salespeople and says at once when a number is already used.

A territory has Edit and Take away together at the top, and two tabs: Companies (now with each company's postal code
and the date it was put in the territory; ticking shows a bar with Clear and Take out of territory) and Add companies,
where Search, Paste a list and Excel workbook are three cards and the companies found are listed as Found and Not
found. Before companies are put in, the question lists those that leave another salesperson.

On a phone the territories and their companies are cards, the switches fit the screen, the ticked companies' bar sits
above the bottom bar and Take away territory is at the bottom of the page.
