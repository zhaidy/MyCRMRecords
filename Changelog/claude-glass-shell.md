# Breadcrumbs and a top bar that folds while you scroll

- A record's page (a company, a costing sheet, a request form, a territory, a machine, a project, machine access) shows
  the whole way you came above its title, for example Costing sheets › Costing Sheet # 23052 › TRUE COLOURS PRINT HOUSE
  PTE. LTD.; every step can be clicked, and a company opens again on the tab you left it on. On a phone only the step
  back is shown.
- Scrolling down folds the menu to the page you are on and shows the page's title in the top bar; scrolling up brings
  the menu back. What scrolls under the bar is blurred.
- Your name and **Sign out** are now behind your initials at the right of the top bar.
